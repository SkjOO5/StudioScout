## 🎬 Act-by-Act Speaking & Screenplay Script

### ACT 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual on Screen:** Show StudioScout AI Landing Page / Control Center with Dark/Light theme active.

  > *"Film production planning turns a raw screenplay into dozens of high-stakes research and logistics decisions. ### ACT 2: Screenplay Ingestion & Multi-Query Agent (0:25 – 0:55)
- **Visual on Screen:** Click **"New Ingestion"** → Click **"Explore Demo ("Cipher Zero")"** → Click **"Launch Autonomous Scout"**.
- **Narrator (Spoken):**
  > *"I'll give StudioScout a screenplay — here, our sci-fi cyber thriller 'Cipher Zero'. Our Google Gemini agent extracts structured scene specs, characters, stunt vehicle requirements, and physical constraints. When the agent determines external intelligence is needed, it autonomously dispatches targeted multi-queries to the Parallel Search API to discover verified real-world filming locations."*

---

### ACT 3: Source-Grounded Location Intelligence & 3D Map (0:55 – 1:35)
- **Visual on Screen:** Show Scene 3 (Turbine Hall / Mukesh Mills). Hover over the 6-dimension match score (94.5/100) → Click **"Verified Evidence"** showing real Parallel Search quotes and source URLs → Open the **Interactive 3D Production Map**.
- **Narrator (Spoken):**
  > *"Instead of giving an ungrounded hallucination, StudioScout evaluates real web results across an explainable 6-dimension rubric: visual match, square footage, vehicle access, power supply, and safety risks. Notice these verified citations: real quotes, source links, and permit notes pulled directly from Parallel Search. On our 3D production map, spatial coordinates and DP camera paths are mapped in real time."*

---

### ACT 4: The Killer Feature — Autonomous Replanning (1:35 – 2:15)
- **Visual on Screen:** Navigate to **"Production Plan"** tab → Click **"Modify Constraints"** → Type: *"Mukesh Mills unavailable on Saturday due to maintenance"* → Click **"Execute Autonomous Re-Plan"**.
- **Narrator (Spoken):**
  > *"Now for the killer feature: autonomous replanning. In real filmmaking, constraints change constantly. If Mukesh Mills becomes unavailable on Saturday, I inject that constraint. The agent instantly invalidates the affected candidate, re-queries Parallel Search for viable industrial alternatives like Kanjurmarg Power Compound, recalculates call times, and updates the entire production schedule without restarting the project."*

## 💡 Top Tips for Screen Recording
1. **Screen Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) in full screen.
2. **Browser:** Zoom at 100% or 110% for crisp typography.
3. **Theme:** Start in Dark Mode, switch to Light Mode briefly during the Export Hub step to demonstrate design versatility.
4. **Live URL:** Display your live URL `https://studioscout-ai-111556269084.asia-south1.run.app` in the video description and final slide.
